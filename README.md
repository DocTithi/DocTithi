## Clinical AI Reliability

My current open-source project is **Clinical AI Reliability (CAR)**, an experimental framework for creating structured reliability records around medical and health AI outputs.

CAR focuses on:

- claim-level evidence tracking
- evidence freshness
- uncertainty reporting
- counterfactual consistency testing
- privacy-minimizing provenance
- human-review triggers
- reproducible AI evaluation
- machine-readable Reliability Envelopes

CAR is research infrastructure. It does not diagnose patients, replace clinical judgment, or certify AI systems as medically safe.

### Current release

**CAR v0.1.0** is available as an early public research pre-release.
